Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about Realty Utsav 2026.

Is entry to Realty Utsav free?

Yes, entry to Realty Utsav 2026 is completely free for all visitors. Simply register through this website to get your free entry pass.

What types of properties will be available?

You can explore budget-friendly homes, premium apartments, luxury villas, plots and more from 25+ leading builders across Trivandrum.

Can I meet builders directly at the event?

Yes. Builders and their project representatives will be present at their stalls to discuss your requirements, pricing and site visits.

Will home loan assistance be available?

Yes. 4+ banks will be at the venue to guide you on eligibility, documentation and applicable interest rates.

Can I attend with my family?

Absolutely. Realty Utsav is a family-friendly event — bring your family along to explore and decide together.

Where and when is the event happening?

31 October & 1 November 2026 at Hyatt Regency, CV Raman Pillai Road, Thycaud, Thiruvananthapuram.

How do I register for the event?

Fill in the registration form on this page with your name, mobile number, preferred property type and budget. You can also call 8606 95 95 95.
